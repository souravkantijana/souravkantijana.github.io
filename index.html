About Me

Connecting physics, chemistry, and machine learning

I am a computational physicist and materials researcher with expertise in atomistic simulation, electronic-structure calculations, and molecular modelling. My research combines quantum-mechanical calculations, classical molecular dynamics, and emerging machine-learning methods to understand interactions, stability, transport, adsorption, and functional behaviour in complex materials.

I completed my Ph.D. in Physics at The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda. I later carried out postdoctoral research at IAMS, Academia Sinica, Taiwan, and Kasetsart University, Thailand. I am currently working as an Assistant Professor in Physics while continuing active research in machine-learning potentials, polymers, MOFs, and energy materials.

Research Areas

Selected research directions

01

Machine-Learning Force Fields

Development and application of MACE and related machine-learning interatomic potentials for efficient atomistic simulations.

02

CO₂ Capture and Separation

Polymer- and MOF-based materials for CO₂ capture, adsorption, diffusion, selectivity, and transport using classical and ML-driven MD.

03

Energy and Functional Materials

Thermal, mechanical, electronic, and interfacial behaviour of two-dimensional materials, MXenes, battery materials, and polymer electrolytes.

04

Biomolecules and Drug Delivery

Adsorption of drugs and biomolecules on carbon nanomaterials, polymer–drug systems, molecular recognition, and nanocarrier interactions.

05

Gas Sensing and Nanomaterials

DFT-based investigation of gas adsorption, charge transfer, electronic structure, and sensing response in pristine and doped nanostructures.

06

Computational Study of Lithium-Ion Electrolytes

Investigating the structure, ion transport, and diffusion mechanisms of lithium-ion battery electrolytes using classical molecular dynamics simulations to understand electrolyte behavior and support the design of safer, high-performance energy storage materials.

Current Projects

Ongoing research themes

Polymer-based drug delivery

Exploring polymer-based nanocarriers using molecular dynamics simulations to understand drug–polymer interactions, stability, and controlled drug release for the development of efficient and targeted drug delivery systems.

Study of thermal and mechanical signatures of MXenes using first-principles data and machine-learning force fields.
